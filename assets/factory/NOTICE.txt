Groovebox Factory Library revision 2

The original 16 DSP drum/percussion WAVs in this directory are generated by
tools/content/factory.cpp and dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0.

Additional factory audio is generated by tools/content/build_factory_library.py
from the exact upstream commits and Git blob hashes in
tools/content/factory_sources.lock.json. The per-file source, license URL,
conversion gain, peak, RMS, and output SHA-256 are in library.json.
Eight drum loops are new arrangements of these licensed one-shots.
Sixteen packed multisample WAVs combine selected instrument zones without
duplicating them per keyboard note.

Upstream recordings and their stated licenses:
* tidalcycles/sounds-tr808-fischer: CC0-1.0, recorded TR-808 sounds.
* octalmusic/octal-samples: CC0-1.0, generated 808/909-style sounds.
* oramics/sampled CR-78, LM-2, and TR-505 collections: Public Domain as stated
  by each collection README. No other oramics collections are included.
* sgossner/VCSL: CC0-1.0, selected percussion and instrument recordings.
* sgossner/VSCO-2-CE: CC0-1.0, selected instrument recordings; credit to
  Versilian Studios and the VSCO 2 CE contributors is appreciated.

Kit names such as 909 Classic, 606 Acid, 707 House and DMX Drumtraks identify
musical style or pad arrangement. They do not imply vendor ROM audio or an
authentic recording of each named drum machine. 303-Acid-Bass and
303-Resonant are presets of this project's Acid engine, not TB-303 ROM data.

Factory content is separate from user recordings. Build with
make factory-library factory-content factory-check, then prepare the SD
boot tree with scripts/prepare-sd.sh.
