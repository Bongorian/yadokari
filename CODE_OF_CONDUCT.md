# Code of conduct

YADOKARI is a small, joyful project. Keep it that way:

- Be decent to each other. Critique code, sounds and ideas as
  hard as you like; don't make it personal.
- No harassment, discrimination or spam, in issues, PRs or
  anywhere the project gathers.
- Assume good faith first — most friction here is musicians and
  engineers talking past each other, not malice.

The maintainer may remove comments, close threads or block
accounts that break these simple rules, and is the final word on
enforcement. If something feels wrong, raise it privately through
the contact on the GitHub profile.

That's the whole policy. Play nice, make noise.
