# Factory sounds and produced content

The current format is MGXC13, with **163 Presets + 24 Kits = 187 items, pending 0**. Of these, 158 Presets + 24 Kits are revision 2 under the same GUIDs; the 5 Presets that needed no change keep their bytes and revision 1. All 187 originals and `identities.json` are as specified.

The source factory catalog is revision 13 (host only). The debug `demo-slices.wav` is separated into `FACTORY/DEBUG`, and same-named WAVs on user shelves are excluded from restore targets and blanket protection. Compatible WAVs on old user shelves are not erased, and the 187 Library originals are invariant. The required count remains 243 files (operational/debug 56 + Library 187) plus 1 optional Kit. The 122 compatible assets and their provenance are retained, and factory sounds are not automatically filled into the pool's 256 slots. Placement is FACTORY/PRESETS/LIBRARY and FACTORY/KITS/LIBRARY. TX81z Lately Bass is the current name; removal of the old name DX7 Lately Bass targets only exact-match paths inside the factory. Same-named copies on user shelves are not changed.

## Production and import

The entry points are the [machine schema](reference/engine-schema.json), the assignments in `assets/factory-library/identities.json`, and `pending_factory_import`. New items go into PRESETS / KITS in the same folder; new material goes into ASSETS with its CRC and relative hint set. The identity of an original Preset/Kit is maintained and kept separate from the Track names of songs.

The `mgxs-converter`'s `make environment` newly generates reference sounds, kits, and songs in the new format and checks VERIFY, the canonical round trip, and material CRCs. Reference User GUIDs are not diverted to the Factory. Produced items are generated only with the current codec; headers of old files are not rewritten. On a partial import, only the items actually imported are removed from pending.

## Retained material and provenance

- The 122 assets of the [asset manifest](../assets/factory-library/manifest.json) are retained for compatibility.
- The provenance and licenses of the original WAVs and the like are kept in the [pinned sources of the public assets](../tools/content/factory_sources.lock.json) and `assets/factory/library.json`. Third-party licenses/notices are not deleted.
- The 6 WAVs in `assets/factory-synth-drums/DRUMS` are offline renders from the current VA/FM. The original recipes are in [synth_drum_recipes.h](../src/synth_drum_recipes.h). 7 old containers are explicitly marked in the manifest as awaiting re-creation.
- The pool has 256 slots, in which users register the definitions their songs need. There is no automatic filling to line up the Factory.

The Factory on DATA is a restorable cache; user save destinations are PRESETS / KITS / PROJECTS. Cleanup from now on is limited to under FACTORY. Current/future containers and compatible material are retained.

## Packaging and scope of checks

Without re-encoding the originals, derivatives are generated by `factory_library_pack.py --write` → `factory_library_bank.py --write` → `make factory-catalog` → `factory_boot_manifest.py --write --revision 13`. `make factory-library-pack-check` verifies the two-stage reversible compression and SHA256. Restoration runs on the boot/file-service side; no expansion or file I/O is added to render processing.
